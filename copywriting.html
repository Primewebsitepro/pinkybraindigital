FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to the questions businesses ask us most.

Do you write in our existing tone of voice?

Yes. We learn how your business communicates and match it, or help define a tone if you don’t have one yet.

Can you write and optimise for SEO at the same time?

Yes, we write for people first but structure content to support your target keywords and search visibility.

How many revisions are included?

We include revision rounds as standard so the final copy feels exactly right before delivery.

Can you write for a technical or niche industry?

Yes, we research thoroughly and can work with your team’s input to get technical details right.

Do you write blog content too?

Yes, ongoing blog and content writing is covered under our content marketing service, which pairs well with copywriting.
